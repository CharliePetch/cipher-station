The station advertises one public URL in public.json and over IPNS. Use the Cloudflare quick tunnel (URL rotates on restart) or set a permanent URL (named tunnel or your own domain/DNS pointing at this station).

Changes take effect after a station restart. Run: sudo systemctl restart cipherstation
IPFS storage cap

Disk space dedicated to Cipher Station's IPFS repo (Datastore.StorageMax). Examples: 10GB, 50GB, 500GB.

The cap applies at garbage-collection time; restart the IPFS daemon to re-read it now: sudo systemctl restart ipfs
